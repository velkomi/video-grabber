# VideoGrabber referrals and promotions

Owner approved implementation on 2026-10-08 following `artifacts/referral-research-20261008/RESEARCH.md`. Defaults: one-level referral, 10% first-payment friend discount, referrer earns 10% actual externally paid net amount, 14-day hold, 365 days validity from availability; no withdrawal/transfer. One best promotion (referral or coupon), then bonus spending subject to an overall 30% reduction from catalog price. Normal public coupons at most 20%. RUB/XTR balances remain separate; Stars recurring invoices do not receive one-time discounts. Qualified products are paid subscription plans; credits/top-ups do not qualify. Only the first successful purchase qualifies; canceled attempts do not consume eligibility.

Server is authoritative. Freeze price/product/rule snapshot at payment creation. Coupon and wallet reservations commit/release with verified provider events in one DB transaction. Hold pending reservations until verified terminal state; never release from a blind timer. Duplicate provider/refund events must not double-grant/spend. Full/partial refunds reduce rewards by verified payment ID, not email. Already-used returned rewards offset future bonus earnings, not a cash debt. Bonus expiry does not invalidate an already-created invoice.

Use existing API/PostgreSQL, shared account and RLS. Feature flag `VG_REFERRALS_ENABLED` defaults false; local tests enable it. No new product packages/containers. Guest Telegram referral intent survives account linking; self-referrals including merged identities are rejected. Source accounts with bonus financial history cannot be silently merged. Admin actions require existing owner authorization and fresh MFA. Referral statistics contain no friends' email or identity data. Logs contain neither codes nor input values.

UI: Studio styling, invite/copy/share buttons, available/pending balances, history, promo quote breakdown. Bot `/referral`, `/bonus`, `/promo`, keyboard toggle preserved. Windows provides matching account card and links into the website for purchases. Work is reversible source changes and isolated tests; production migration/activation and release are separate reviewable operations. No live payment/message/test orders.

Clarification: the first externally paid INITIAL recurring subscription may reward its referrer; subsequent renewals do not. One-time price reductions are not applied to recurring invoices. Verified full refund restores spent payer bonuses with a new365-day validity, preserving returned value even if its original lot expired while the invoice was pending; no external cash value is created.
